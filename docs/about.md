Pitstop surfaces the nearest open restaurant, supermarket, fuel station, cafe, hotel, doctor, pharmacy, bike shop, ATM, train station, and water refill along your planned route, and routes you there in two taps via the Karoo's built-in navigation.

Plan a route on the Karoo as usual; Pitstop fetches POIs from OpenStreetMap over Wi-Fi and renders them as data field tiles on your ride profile page. Each tile shows the nearest non-closed POI per category with distance, name, and opening status. Tap a tile to open the Karoo pin activity and either Navigate or Save as POI.
